Single-column chat in the same rhythm as wise-signal.html onboarding — round avatar + tinted bubble, every message on the left, no right-aligned bubbles. The AI's opening bubble hosts intent chips inline after the "Try an intent" label; tapping a chip fires it as the user's reply, the chips clear, and the AI responds. New messages anchor to the bottom so the conversation grows upward. The composer pins to the bottom; the header kebab opens a new / share / export menu on tap. Sends post to /api/code/compose in the live app; the response hydrates the slot list on Screen 1.
